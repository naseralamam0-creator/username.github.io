Ingredients:

1½ cups plain flour
1 cup caster sugar
½ cup cocoa powder
1 tsp baking soda
½ tsp salt
2 eggs
1 cup milk
½ cup vegetable oil
1 tsp vanilla extract

Method:

Preheat oven to 180°C and grease a cake tin.
In a bowl, sift flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt to remove lumps.
Add sugar and mix evenly.
In another bowl, whisk eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla.
Combine wet and dry mixtures, stirring until smooth (do not overmix).
Pour batter into tin and level the surface.
Bake for 30–35 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean.
Allow to cool before removing and decorating.

Extra Tips:

Don’t overmix → keeps cake soft and fluffy
You can add chocolate icing or ganache for flavour
Let it cool fully before cutting
