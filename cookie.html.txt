Ingredients:

1 cup plain flour
½ cup sugar (brown or white)
½ cup butter (softened)
1 egg
1 tsp vanilla extract
½ tsp baking soda
½ cup chocolate chips

Method:

Preheat oven to 180°C and line a tray with baking paper.
Cream butter and sugar together until light and smooth.
Add egg and vanilla, mix well.
In another bowl, combine flour and baking soda.
Gradually mix dry ingredients into wet mixture.
Fold in chocolate chips evenly.
Scoop small portions onto tray (leave space).
Bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are golden.
Let cookies cool (they firm up after baking).

Extra Tips:

Don’t overbake → keeps cookies soft
Use brown sugar for chewier texture
Cookies continue cooking slightly after removal
