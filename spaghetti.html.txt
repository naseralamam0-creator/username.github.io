Ingredients:

200g dried spaghetti
2 cups tomato pasta sauce
1 small onion (finely chopped)
2 cloves garlic (crushed)
200g minced beef (optional)
1 tbsp olive oil
Salt and pepper
Optional: herbs (basil/oregano), grated cheese

Method:

Bring a pot of salted water to boil and cook spaghetti for 8–10 mins until al dente.
Heat oil in a pan and sauté onion until soft.
Add garlic and cook briefly (avoid burning).
Add minced meat and cook until browned.
Pour in tomato sauce, add seasoning and herbs.
Simmer for 10–15 minutes to develop flavour.
Drain pasta and combine with sauce or serve on top.

Extra Tips:

Salt the water → improves pasta taste
Don’t overcook pasta (slightly firm = best)
Add cheese on top for extra flavour
